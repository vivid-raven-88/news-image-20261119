A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of
**Thursday 19 November 2026**, entered in the make.fun competition
[Sum up 19 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

## The day in four stories

1. **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — the first three-way summit
   of the US, Chinese and Russian presidents, on the sidelines of the
   APEC forum.
2. **Iran strikes Gulf bases as the US hits Qeshm** — Iran fired at a US
   air base in Kuwait and Bahrain after US strikes on a Revolutionary
   Guard missile site on Qeshm Island; a tanker was hit in the Strait
   of Hormuz.
3. **World court rules on the Myanmar genocide case** — the
   International Court of Justice delivers its judgment on claims of
   genocide against Myanmar's Rohingya minority.
4. **Jeffries set to be first Black Speaker of the House** — Democrat
   Hakeem Jeffries is set to become the first African-American Speaker
   of the US House of Representatives.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the front page
`entry.png` — drawn in this repository's session — to
`/output/entry.png`. It runs with no network and writes the same bytes
on every run.
